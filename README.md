# Harmoniq
Harmoniq is a music player app developed in Swift. It provides an intuitive and user-friendly interface for playing and managing your music collection.

# Features
Play music: Seamlessly play your favorite tracks.

Create playlists: Organize your music into custom playlists.

Search: Quickly find songs in your library.

Shuffle and Repeat: Enjoy your music in shuffle or repeat mode.

User-friendly interface: Easy to navigate and use.
